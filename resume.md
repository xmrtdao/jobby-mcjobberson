# Joseph Andrew Lee
**Multi-Agent Systems Architect, Constitutional AI**

From combat correspondent to constitutional agent architect.

Joseph Andrew Lee is a U.S. Marine Corps veteran (served 1997–2007, enlisted at age 17), award-winning photojournalist, producer and full-stack Web3 developer. He is the founder of XMRT DAO and MobileMonero.com, and serves as a multi-agent systems architect and key contributor to Cuttlefish Labs, where his pioneering work on autonomous, constitutionally-governed agent ecosystems directly informs the Cuttlefishclaws platform and its CAC (Compute Access Certificate) protocol.

**Service & Storytelling:** Marine Corps combat correspondent in Iraq and Afghanistan. His photograph of Kobe Bryant became a Wikipedia Featured Picture used across dozens of language editions and selected as Wikipedia's Picture of the Day on August 24, 2022. After active duty, senior multimedia journalist for the USO in Arlington, VA; work appeared in On Patrol magazine, People Magazine, and other national outlets.

**Harvard & The Pivot:** Master's in Journalism from Harvard University, focused on advanced communication strategies and technology dissemination. Shifted from documenting systems to building them.

**2022–2026 — The Technical Rebirth:** Re-trained as a full-stack engineer and applied that skill set across his own ventures. Founded XMRT Solutions, PCS, LLC and launched the XMRT DAO Ecosystem — the world's first AI-governed, meshnet-powered mobile Monero mining and banking platform. Key components: Eliza (AI governor with LangGraph memory and autonomous feedback loops), XMRT MESHNET (peer-to-peer radio mesh network), MobileMonero.com + CashDapp (mobile-first decentralized banking with no KYC), Night Moves (proof-of-concept turning sleeping Android phones into passive miners). Built and operated live tooling for Party Favor Photo (booking, quoting, contracts) and the Elze Contract Suite (VA commercial lease compliance, redlining, DOCX generation) — full-stack production systems in React/Vite, Node, Postgres, and edge-function architecture.

**Cuttlefish Labs:** Contributes to CAC Protocol (Compute Access Certificates), TrustGraph Integration, Agent Lifecycle Architecture, KYA (Know Your Agent) open-standard identity layer, and the Tributary AI Campus (420,460 SF former AT&T operations center in Birmingham, AL, acquired as a 31M DAO-REIT).

**Philosophy:** Shaped by conviction forged in combat zones and newsrooms — systems should serve people. Privacy is not a luxury. Resilience is not optional. The future belongs to those who build it themselves.
