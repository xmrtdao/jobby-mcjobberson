Inspired by Jobseeker, built for Jobby

Similar branded funnel, tighter scope.

Jobseeker sells one job-search platform: resume, cover letter, jobs, and application tracking. Jobby should feel just as clear, but it must stay honest about its boundaries: it prepares, personalizes, and coordinates; fleet services handle sending and ATS work.

Audit-first automation

Never invent a contact. Never hide a suppression decision.

Every recipient record is normalized, deduplicated, suppressed, and audited before anything can be handed to campaign delivery. Unknown contacts stay unknown.

  • Normalize recipient fields without mutating input.
  • Preserve missing and unverified contacts as unknown.
  • Apply identity, verified-email, and consent-denial suppression.
  • Discard malformed records with audit data.
