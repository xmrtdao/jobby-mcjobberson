## 🔍 Manual Search Strategy

### Method 1: TheKnot.com (10 min/location)

1. Go to: https://www.theknot.com/marketplace/wedding-planners-[location]
2. Browse listings (20-30 per page)
3. Click each planner's profile
4. Copy: Name, Email, Phone, Website
5. Add to spreadsheet

**Estimated:** 20 planners/location × 8 locations = 160 leads
