          What it actually does

          Four things, done properly.

          Most job search tools are a template library and a button. Jobby holds your professional identity in one place, reads it through the lens that job actually needs, and does the repetitive part of the search on your behalf.

          01

          Every version of you

          One dossier, read differently for every kind of work. A career that looks thin to a site recruiter and strong to an editor is not two careers — it is one, and the view is what changes. Nothing is invented to fill a gap; if a view has nothing to show, it says so.

          02

          It finds the work

          Jobby searches for roles that fit what you have actually done, not keywords you once typed. It reads the posting, works out whether you are a real fit, and tells you plainly when you are not.

          03

          It writes and sends

          A message written for that company and that role, from your dossier rather than from a template. You approve it, or you let Jobby send within a daily cap you set. Every message is logged.

          04

          It applies for you

          It fills in the form in your own browser and attaches your CV. If the form asks something your dossier does not answer, it stops and asks you instead of inventing an answer.

          Multi-Track Strategy

          Five tracks. One control plane.

          Track Purpose Current status
          Track 1 High-Engagement Consultancy: Precision outreach for specialized advisory and fractional roles. Active recipient pipeline
          Track 2 Agile Contracting: Streamlined pipelines for temporary and short-term high-impact opportunities. Active recipient pipeline
          Track 3 Strategic Placement: Orchestrated applications for permanent leadership and specialist roles. Active recipient pipeline
          Track 4 ATS Automation: Boundary-aware automation for seamless application delivery via Page-Agent. Page-agent boundary
          Track 5 FIFO & Remote-Site Roles: Fly-in/fly-out and austere-site work across mining, offshore oil and gas, polar stations, remote trades and shipboard roles. Opens on a stated willingness to rotate

          About fly-in/fly-out work

          Rotational roles mean fixed blocks on site — often two weeks on, two off — with camp or bunk accommodation, and contracts that run a season rather than a year. Flights, housing and meals are commonly covered; visa sponsorship is not, and neither is the ordinary part of your life while you are away.

          The roster behind this track is 88 roles. Two things about it you should know before you rely on it: most links point at an employer's careers page or a job board search rather than a specific requisition, so they need finding first; and the pay figures are conversions of local ranges, not numbers any employer quoted. Jobby shows the employer's own wording beside them rather than the converted figure alone.

          This track does not open on a resume that merely shows site experience. Having worked a northern mine says someone can do the work; it does not say they want to be away three weeks at a time, and plenty of people who could are bound by dependants, health, or a partner who cannot travel. Say you want rotations and it opens.

          The part that matters

          It will not make something up.

          An agent that invents a fact about you is worse than no agent, because you find out at the interview. Jobby only states what your resume or your own corrections establish. Anything it cannot confirm is listed as unconfirmed, and anything that looks wrong on your resume is flagged so you can fix it.

          • No employer, title, date or number that is not in your record.
          • Gaps stay gaps until you fill them — Jobby will not close them for you.
          • Every change to your profile is logged with who made it.
          • Every message sent is kept, with the time and the recipient.
          • A daily send cap, and a stop button that takes effect immediately.
