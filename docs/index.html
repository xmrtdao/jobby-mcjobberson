        Architected for Professionalism

        A branded funnel, a tighter scope.

        Jobby provides the critical preparation layer that separates raw leads from successful applications. While fleet services handle the final delivery and ATS interaction, Jobby ensures every outbound signal is normalized, personalized, and audited.

        Maintain a single, canonical version of the professional identity—resume, contact block, and target roles—to ensure absolute consistency across all tracks.

        02

        Track 2 Agile Contracting: Streamlined pipelines for temporary and short-term high-impact opportunities. Active recipient pipeline
        Track 3 Strategic Placement: Orchestrated applications for permanent leadership and specialist roles. Active recipient pipeline

        Audit-First Intelligence

        Never invent a contact. Never hide a decision.

        Professional integrity is our core constraint. Every recipient record is normalized, deduplicated, and audited before reaching the delivery layer. Unknown contacts stay unknown; suppression decisions are transparent and permanent.

        • Strict normalization without mutating source input.
        • Preservation of missing/unverified contacts as unknown.
        • Multi-layer suppression (Identity, Verified Email, and Explicit Consent).
        • Automated discard of malformed records with detailed audit trails.
