For employers

Write the posting once. Have Jobby check it before anyone reads it.

Drop in a job description and Jobby reads it the way a candidate will: it finds the title, the place, the pay and every requirement, and it tells you what it could not read. You fix that before it goes on the board, not after someone has applied to a job that was not really what you offered.

Posting is free. Jobby does not take a percentage of a hire and does not sell your listing to anyone.

Describe the role in your own words and Jobby will draft it, tell you what is missing, and stop where you tell it to. It will not add a requirement you did not say, and it will not turn "preferred" into "required" to make a posting look tighter.
